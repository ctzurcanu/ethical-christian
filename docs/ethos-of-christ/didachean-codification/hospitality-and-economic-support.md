# Hospitality and Economic Support

**Didachean addition — `explicit`**

> `bounded_support`: The community receives travelers and recognized workers, supplies need, and shares firstfruits, while time limits, conduct, work, and the destination of a request protect it from extractive representation.

[Didache 11–13](../../start/didache.md#chapter-11) turns hospitality into a graduated protocol. An apostle's stay is limited; asking personally for money disqualifies a claimant; a traveler receives short-term help; a settler works if able; teachers and prophets may receive food; and firstfruits go to prophets or, where none exists, to the poor. Earlier, [Didache 4:5–8](../../start/didache.md#chapter-4) requires open-handed sharing with a brother and the needy.

The rule attempts to sustain both giver and mission. Need authorizes receipt, while Christian identity alone does not authorize indefinite idleness or private extraction. Support of representatives is tied to work and tested status; support of the poor remains when a representative is absent.

**Theory mapping:** ALS of resources, sustaining and investing, missionary responsibility, representation, temporary person–institution relation. **Open challenge:** ableism, involuntary unemployment, wealth inequality, and the power of hosts over dependent travelers require safeguards not supplied by the code.
