# Local Leadership and Discipline

**Didachean addition — `explicit`**

> `locally_appointed_service_offices`: The community appoints tested bishops and deacons for local ministry and joins their authority to qualifications, peaceful correction, repentance, and Gospel-shaped action.

[Didache 15:1–2](../../start/didache.md#chapter-15) tells the addressees to appoint for themselves bishops and deacons who are meek, truthful, approved, and not lovers of money. Their work continues functions associated with prophets and teachers without merely erasing those itinerant roles. Authority is therefore both locally selected and behaviorally qualified.

[Didache 14:1–2](../../start/didache.md#chapter-14) requires confession and reconciliation before the gathering's offering; [15:3–4](../../start/didache.md#chapter-15) orders peaceful reproof, temporary non-association with an unrepentant wrongdoer, and conformity of communal acts to the Gospel.

The code gives no detailed election procedure, term, appeal, removal mechanism, or response to abusive officers. “Appoint for yourselves” grounds local participation, but its exact electorate and decision rule remain unknown.

**Theory mapping:** governance, servant authority, representation, correction and repair, local institution formation.
