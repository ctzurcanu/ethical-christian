# Virtues

Virtues are a secondary index in this project. They help explain the ordering of ends or capacities required to enact an interaction rule; they do not decide which ethos speaks, who represents it, or what procedure governs another party.

| Virtue or end | Ethos questions it may clarify | Example studies |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Justice | equal measure, evidence, proportional correction, restitution | [Judgment and Hypocrisy](../ethos-of-jesus/proof-witness-and-judgment/judgment-and-hypocrisy.md), [Community Discipline](../pauline-upgrade/community-discipline.md) |
| Governance | legitimate role, delegation, decision, review | [Binding and Loosing](../ethos-of-jesus/governance-and-institution/binding-and-loosing.md), [Jerusalem Settlement](../ethos-of-christ/the-jerusalem-settlement.md) |
| Temperance | use of wealth, restraint of liberty, non-retaliation | [Wealth and Stewardship](../ethos-of-jesus/awareness-and-resources/wealth-and-stewardship.md), [Conscience and Disagreement](../pauline-upgrade/conscience-and-disagreement.md) |
| Charity | allocation toward another's need | [Neighbor and Stranger](../ethos-of-jesus/cooperation-and-compatibility/neighbor-and-stranger.md), [Inter-community Responsibility](../pauline-upgrade/inter-community-responsibility.md) |
| Forgiveness | restoration after acknowledged wrong | [Repair, Repentance, and Forgiveness](../ethos-of-jesus/responsibility/repair-repentance-and-forgiveness.md) |
| Humility | self-examination and refusal of status domination | [Servant Leadership](../ethos-of-jesus/governance-and-institution/servant-leadership.md) |

A page should add a virtue only after specifying whose action is evaluated, which other ends compete, and how the ordering affects the ethos. Labels such as “compassion” or “humility” cannot substitute for a support, judgment, or governance protocol.
