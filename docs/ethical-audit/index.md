# Ethical Audit

The audit tests the reconstructions rather than assuming their coherence. It records which Theory of Ethos attributes are explicit, implied, absent, or contradicted; compares declarations within and across corpora; identifies missing governance or repair protocols; and separates a rule's textual existence from its historical enactment.

An audit result is not a verdict on a person or religion in the abstract. It is a claim about a specified version of an ethos under stated evidence. Each adverse finding must name the conflicting declarations or the declaration–action discrepancy, preserve counter-evidence, and remain open to challenge under the project's status system.
