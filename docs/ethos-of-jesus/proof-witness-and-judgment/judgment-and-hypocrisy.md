# Judgment and Hypocrisy

**Proposed declaration — `explicit`**

> `judgment_is_reciprocal_and_self-applying`: One who judges another becomes answerable to the same measure and must expose and correct the judge's own relevant fault before claiming competence to intervene.

[Matthew 7:1–5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%207%3A1-5&version=ISV) and [Luke 6:37–42](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%206%3A37-42&version=ISV) do not end with permanent blindness toward another's “speck.” They require prior self-examination and warn that the measure used will return upon the judge. The hypocrisy lies in asymmetrical standards and uncorrected impairment.

This constrains but does not abolish judgment. Jesus also commands fruit-testing, private correction, witness-supported hearings, and judgments about teaching. A coherent reconstruction distinguishes condemning status-seeking judgment from accountable evaluation necessary for repair and protection.

**Theory mapping:** mirroring, `judge_invite`, reciprocal declarations, impartiality, no-contradiction claim. **Open challenge:** the Gospel passages do not establish an independent reviewer when the judge cannot recognize the judge's own impairment.

**Secondary virtue note:** humility supports self-examination, while justice governs equal measures. The interaction rule remains primary.
