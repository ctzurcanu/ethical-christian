# The Ethos is Transcendental

**Proposed declaration — `explicit`; proposed qualification — `tentative`**

> `ethos_transcendental`: I accept the transcendental nature of my ethos.

## Witness

appears in:

- [Matthew 17:24-28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2017%3A24-28&version=ISV)

### Quotes

Matthew 17:24-28 `Questions about the Temple Tax`

24 When they came to Capernaum, the collectors of the temple tax came up to Peter and asked, “Your teacher pays the temple tax, doesn’t he?”

25 He answered, “Yes.”

When Peter went home, Jesus spoke to him first and asked him, “What do you think, Simon? From whom do kings on the earth collect tolls or tributes? From their own subjects, or from foreigners?”

## Beyond Natural Laws

An ethos is formed and exists beyond creation.

## Theory Mapping

`positive_reciprocity`, action in the name of a fully-developed ethos, and ethos–ethos treatment.

## Ethical Lessons

- an ethos is a transcendental entity
- an ethos has its own natural laws
- an ethos has its own laws beyond creation
- an ethos can perform miracles: transfers from its transcendental part to the created part
- the miracles of an ethos should be clear and provable
- the accounting of an ethos is also within and beyond creation
- an ethos can exchange funds with any other ethos (for example with the state)
- an ethos has its own currency and economy
- an ethos is like a kingdom:
  - has its own hierarchy
  - has sovereignty in at least one respect
  - has dominion on ~
- volunteers for an ethos are sheltered by the ethos
- the expenses of some volunteers are paid by the ethos
- the godly ethos can make prophecy and manufacture omens
- the representatives of an ethos can perform miracles
- an ethos recognizes and tries to accommodate other established ethoses
- an ethos is generous
- an ethos can and should pay (with its services) in advance its volunteers

## Indirect Ethical Lessons

- an ethos also can transfer from the created part to its transcendental part
