# Ethos as Ordered Logos

## Witness

appears in:

- [John 1:51](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%201%3A51&version=KJV)
- [Genesis 28:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%2028%3A12&version=KJV)


### Quotes

[John 1:51](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%201%3A51&version=KJV)

51 And he saith unto him, Verily, verily, I say unto you, Hereafter ye shall see heaven open, and the angels of God ascending and descending upon the Son of man.

[Genesis 28:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%2028%3A12&version=KJV) `Jacob’s Ladder `

12 And he dreamed, and behold a ladder set up on the earth, and the top of it reached to heaven: and behold the angels of God ascending and descending on it.


## Consequential Details

- the Greek word used for "upon" is epi ἐπί: on, upon, over, on the basis of
- many English Bible versions have the word "ἐπί" mistranslated ([International Standard Version](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%201%3A51&version=ISV): "ἐπί" = "to")
- a ladder has to cover the whole spectrum and has to define a clear hierarchy
- an example of a spiritual ladder: [the hierarchy of virtues](https://virtues.provable.dev/docs/hierarchies#virtues-order)
- a complete ethos also has to define the order of all accepted virtues: [example of complete ethos](https://ctzurcanu.github.io/ethos/ethos#virtues)


## Ethical Lessons

- a complete ethos defines the ordering of the Logos
- an ethos defines the ordered set of virtues
- Jesus/Christ/Son of Man is the human form of an ethos
- Jesus/Christ/Son of Man is the human form of an ordered Logos

## Indirect Ethical Lessons

- some humans can represent an ethos completely
