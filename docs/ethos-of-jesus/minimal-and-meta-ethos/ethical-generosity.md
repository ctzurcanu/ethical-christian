# Ethical Generosity

## Witness

appears in:

- [Matthew 5:39](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%205%3A39%20&version=ISV)
- [Luke 6:29](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%206%3A29&version=ISV)


### Quotes

[Matthew 5:39](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%205%3A39%20&version=ISV) `The Sermon on the Mount`

39 But I tell you not to resist an evildoer. On the contrary, whoever slaps you on the right cheek, turn the other to him as well.

[Luke 6:29](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%206%3A29&version=ISV) `The Sermon on the Plain`

29 If someone strikes you on the cheek, offer him the other one as well, and if someone takes your coat, don’t keep back your shirt, either.


## Consequential Details

- action is demanded, not passive acceptance


## Ethical Lessons

- ethical generosity is asted while inhabiting an ethos, not as an individual
- this has ethical effect especially if the first actor (the abuser) is inhabiting an ethos too


## Indirect Ethical Lessons

- this would work best if witnesses present or a record is created
- an ethos has the duty to record such events
