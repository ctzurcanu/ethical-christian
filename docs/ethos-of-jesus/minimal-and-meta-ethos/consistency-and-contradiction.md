# Consistency and Contradiction

**Proposed declaration — `tentative`**

> `consistency_by_context`: Apparently opposed sayings must first be tested for differences in actor, action, scope, and moment; genuine contradictions remain attached to the reconstruction if those differences do not resolve them.

The Gospels do not quote Jesus declaring a formal `no_contradiction_claim`. They do, however, use coherence tests: a divided kingdom cannot stand, fruit discloses the tree, and doing must match saying. Those tests justify auditing Jesus' attributed teaching for contradiction without presuming in advance that none exists.

Two early test cases are “not against us is for us” ([Mark 9:40](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Mark%209%3A40&version=ISV)) and “not with me is against me” ([Matthew 12:30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2012%3A30&version=ISV)). The first concerns an unaffiliated actor doing a work in Jesus' name; the second concerns participation in Jesus' own contested work. Context may distinguish compatible external action from refusal at a decisive interaction, but that is a proposed resolution, not a license to harmonize every tension.

Other audits concern visible works versus secret practice, honoring parents versus leaving family, non-judgment versus communal exclusion, and restricted versus widened mission. Each receives a status and counter-evidence rather than being solved by appeal to canonical unity.
