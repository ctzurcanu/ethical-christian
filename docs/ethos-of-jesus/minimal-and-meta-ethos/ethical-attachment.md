## Consequential Details

- Lot's wife was allowed to escape Sodom under different conditions than Lot because she was not of the same ethos as Lot: Lot interceded for her.
- The same idea is illustrated by the myth of Orpheus and Eurydice
- Christianity requires a high degree of detachment from the ordinary life



## Ethical Lessons

- an ethos can accept guests and refugees
- under some conditions
- changing ethos requires a period of hygienic seclusion or separation
- ability to have an ethos is dependent on Temperance and control of desires and habits


## Indirect Ethical Lessons

- looking back is also a metaphor: it is enough to have enough attachment to the old life or ethos to not be ready for admission into a more elevated ethos
