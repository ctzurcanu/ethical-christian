## Consequential Details

- Jesus pays the Temple tax only for Peter and himself
- The Temple tax is payable for Peter: he was at least 20 years old

| Event | If crucifixion = **7 Apr AD 30** | If crucifixion = **3 Apr AD 33** |
|---|---:|---:|
| Temple-tax collection season begins | ~late Feb AD 30 | ~late Feb AD 33 |
| **Temple-tax incident** | **early–mid March 30** | **early–mid March 33** |

- It seems to follow that all other disciples were younger than 20 at that time: 2 months prior to crucifixion
- It was a great apparent difference in age between Peter and John
- John would not even enter rooms before Peter out of deference
- at that point in history, as a man (single bread earner), you could not leave your family
- also as a small child (before bar mitzvah) you would not be able to leave the house of your parents
- but after 12-13: there was a window when you could travel away from the habitual life
- boys married at 16-18


## Ethical Lessons

- the age of ethical formation is between 13 (bar/bat mitzvah) and 21
- very probable John was 13 when he became a disciple and Peter 19-20


## Indirect Ethical Lessons

- proposed timeline for ethics:
  - 0-7 years of age: learning control of our body. Ethos is applied from the exterior
  - 7-14: contest, cooperation with others
  - 14-21: formation, discovery of internal ethos
  - 21-28: contest, cooperation of ethos: applied ethics. Ethos is applied from the interior
