# Present Audit

At this stage, the reconstruction has substantial evidence for a minimal identity, missionary responsibility, awareness of mission resources, challenge through questions and correction, partial cooperation with outsiders, acceptance of witnesses, behavioral proof, delegated representation, and servant governance.

Important gaps remain. The Gospels do not present a single public register of declarations; the route for amending the whole ethos is unclear; procedures differ among witnesses; reparations are commanded more clearly than institutional liability is assigned; and post-death baptism, offices, and inter-community government must not be imported into Jesus' lifetime. Apparent tensions concerning outsiders, publicity, family, law, judgment, and departure are still open.

The proper status is therefore **provisional reconstruction**, not “ethically complete ethos.” Completeness can be claimed only after the phylogeny matrix and contradiction audit identify the evidence for every required attribute and answer the strongest counterexamples.
