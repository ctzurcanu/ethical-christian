# Actions

Actions supply enacted evidence but require the same caution as sayings. A narrated act may prove a declared rule, expose resource use, establish a representative relation, or challenge an institution; it is not automatically a universal command.

| Action | Ethical use | Primary location |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Jesus forms and sends disciples | formation, authorization, reporting | [Formation of the Disciples](../ethos-of-jesus/governance-and-institution/formation-of-the-disciples.md) |
| Jesus washes disciples' feet | leader enacts service before requiring it | [Servant Leadership](../ethos-of-jesus/governance-and-institution/servant-leadership.md) |
| Jesus heals amid Sabbath dispute | welfare tests legal interpretation | [Sabbath and Law](../ethos-of-jesus/flexibility-and-challenge/sabbath-and-law.md) |
| Jesus responds to a Gentile woman's challenge | audience boundary and responsiveness | [Geography, Audience, and Time](../ethos-of-jesus/awareness-and-resources/geography-audience-and-time.md) |
| Disciples leave in John 6 | factual exit from discipleship | [Joining and Leaving](../ethos-of-jesus/cooperation-and-compatibility/joining-and-leaving.md) |
| Jerusalem assembly redistributes resources | corporate responsibility and allocation | [Apostolic Enactment](../ethos-of-christ/apostolic-enactment.md) |
| Jerusalem representatives negotiate Gentile entry | translocal challenge and settlement | [The Jerusalem Settlement](../ethos-of-christ/the-jerusalem-settlement.md) |
| Paul coordinates a collection | responsibility between communities | [Inter-community Responsibility](../pauline-upgrade/inter-community-responsibility.md) |

The actor, authorization, beneficiary, cost, alternatives, result, and narrator's interpretation are recorded separately before an ethical declaration is inferred.
