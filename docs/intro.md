# The Ethical New Testament

This project reconstructs the ethoses attested by the New Testament and the early Christian sources needed to understand their transmission. Here, *ethical* means pertaining to the interaction of ethoses: their declarations, members, representatives, institutions, judgments, resources, conflicts, and forms of cooperation.

The argument begins with the ethos enacted and taught by Jesus, then keeps distinct the ethos shared in the name of Christ after Jesus' death, the Didachean codification of that shared life, the Pauline upgrade, later upgrades, and present popular Christian ethoses. Virtues remain a secondary explanatory layer rather than the structure of the project.
