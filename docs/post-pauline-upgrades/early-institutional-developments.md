# Early Institutional Developments

**Research status — `not yet reconstructed`**

This layer begins where communities after the Pauline corpus stabilize structures that earlier witnesses leave fluid or local. Candidate changes include more durable episcopal, presbyteral, and diaconal offices; translocal recognition of teachers and texts; extended catechesis; formal treatment of schism and readmission; support rules for clergy and poor members; and clearer boundaries around the communal meal.

Each candidate must be studied from dated primary witnesses rather than narrated as inevitable “growth.” The required comparison is:

```text
earlier problem and protocol
→ later institution claiming authority
→ new membership or representation rule
→ enforcement and remedy
→ observable effect on members and outsiders
```

The Didache is the baseline for several questions because it still places itinerant prophets beside locally appointed bishops and deacons. Later concentration of authority counts as an upgrade only descriptively; whether it improves proof, service, and accountability is an audit result.

The Pastoral Epistles, 1 Clement, Ignatius, Polycarp, the Shepherd of Hermas, Justin, and early church orders are initial source groups. Their chronology, authorship, location, and institutional vocabulary must remain attached to each claim.
